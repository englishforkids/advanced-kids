# Lesson 31 — промпти для власних кадрів

Зараз усі фони зроблені з кадрів відео учнів. Якщо захочете окремі реалістичні кадри 16:9 — ось промпти.
Однаковий стиль для всіх: *cinematic still from a teen drama, realistic photo, warm practical lights, shallow depth of field, 16:9, no text*.

**Leo** — 15, curly brown hair, blue zip hoodie over a blue T-shirt, friendly, a bit shy.
**Mia** — 15, dark hair in a messy bun, lilac sweatshirt, outgoing, expressive.
**The city** — a detailed miniature city made of cardboard: small houses, streets, shops with awnings, tiny cars, trees, warm LED streetlights.

| Файл | Промпт |
|---|---|
| `scenes/title.webp` | Leo’s bedroom at night, a huge detailed cardboard miniature city on his desk glowing with tiny LED streetlights, Leo leaning over it, purple ambient light |
| `scenes/bedroom.webp` | Leo standing proudly behind his cardboard city in his bedroom, warm lamp light, posters on the wall |
| `scenes/discover.webp` | Mia stepping into Leo’s room and seeing the cardboard city for the first time, hand over her mouth, amazed; Leo smiling shyly |
| `scenes/morning.webp` | early morning, Leo in bed staring at his phone in disbelief, the miniature city on the desk in soft daylight |
| `scenes/storm.webp` | close-up of a phone screen full of comments, reflections on Leo’s worried face, cool blue light |
| `scenes/reading.webp` | Leo sitting at his desk next to the city, writing in a notebook, thoughtful |
| `scenes/decision.webp` | overhead view of the illuminated cardboard city at night, three small paths of light leading in different directions |
| `scenes/yours.webp` | a group of teenagers with different hobbies (skateboard, camera, sketchbook, Rubik’s cube) in a bright school corridor |
| `scenes/mission.webp` | Leo and Mia filming a short vertical video of the city with a phone on a tiny tripod |
| `scenes/ending.webp` | Leo in his bedroom adding a new tiny building to the city, warm golden light, peaceful smile |
| `scenes/game.webp` | playful flat lay: cardboard houses, a phone, a Rubik’s cube, a sketchbook, sneakers on a dark desk |

Розмір — 1600×900 або більше, формат `.webp`. Назва файлу та сама → нічого в коді міняти не треба.

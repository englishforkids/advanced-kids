The Perfect Night?

Evidence document · 210 words

At 8:30 p.m., Alex dropped a post from the school party. The photo looked perfect: everyone was smiling, the lights were bright, and Alex's clothes looked amazing. The caption said, "Best night ever!"

However, the photo wasn't as natural as it seemed. Alex had a friend take more than fifty pictures before choosing one. Then Alex got an older sister to edit the final photo. She made the lights brighter, removed several objects and changed everyone's faces a little.

At first, Alex was excited. The post quickly got hundreds of likes. Some students who hadn't attended the party felt FOMO. They thought they had missed an incredible event.

But one person in the photo, Jamie, wasn't happy. Jamie had asked Alex not to share the picture. Alex had also made Jamie stand next to people they didn't really know. When Jamie saw the heavily edited photo, they sent Alex a message: "This isn't us. It's only a highlight reel."

Alex read the message while doomscrolling through the comments. Suddenly, the perfect post didn't feel post-worthy anymore. Alex deleted it and wrote to Jamie: "You're right. Next time, I'll try to show up real."

CLUE 3Core6–7 min

Who Really Did It?

The reading contains clues about who caused each action. Let's investigate the structures.

У тексті є підказки про те, хто спричинив кожну дію. Дослідімо ці конструкції.

1. Something done FOR you

have / get + object + V3

We use this structure when another person does something for us. We arrange the action, but we do not do it ourselves.

Ми використовуємо цю конструкцію, коли інша людина щось робить для нас. Ми організовуємо дію, але не виконуємо її самі.
"Alex got the final photo edited."

Alex didn't edit it. Alex's sister did it.

Get something done is usually more informal than have something done.

2. Asking or persuading someone

have + person + V
get + person + to + V

Use have when you ask or tell someone to do something. Use get when you ask, persuade or convince someone to do something.

Використовуємо have, коли просимо або доручаємо комусь виконати дію. Використовуємо get, коли вмовляємо або переконуємо когось виконати дію.
"Alex had a friend take fifty pictures."
"Alex got a sister to edit the photo."
Memory tip: geT → To
Get someone to do something.
Have has no final T → no to. Have someone do something.

3. Pressure or no real choice

make + person + V

Use make when someone is forced or strongly pressured to do something.

Використовуємо make, коли когось змушують або сильно тиснуть на нього, щоб він виконав дію.
"Alex made Jamie stand near the group."

After make, we do not use to.

Grammar practice

Ask yourself two questions: 1) is the second word a person or a thing? 2) is the verb have, get or make? Only get + person takes to.

Grammar summary

have/get + thing + V3 → someone does it for you
have + person + V → ask or tell someone
get + person + to + V → persuade someone
make + person + V → force or strongly pressure someone
